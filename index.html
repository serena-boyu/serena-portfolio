Serena Ng — Product Designer

Serena Ng is a product-obsessed designer who believes good design is never finished. She currently works on patient experience and AI workflows at Epic Systems, and previously designed for Ronik Design Agency, Snyk, JFK Airport, and Reuters.

She has worn a lot of hats, including product research, UX/UI, visual design, development, and marketing, and has worked shoulder-to-shoulder with engineers, marketers, and clients across startups and agencies. She believes the best solutions rarely stay inside one discipline, and cares most about solving the right problem: the one with the most impact, and the one that holds up as the product grows.
