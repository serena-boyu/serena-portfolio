How this portfolio was built

Serena designed this portfolio in Figma, then built it with Anthropic's Claude — working iteratively in conversation to turn the designs into a live, responsive site — and deployed it herself through GitHub Pages on a custom domain.

  • Figma to live site in 7 days
  • Optimized for humans and AI
  • Dark mode shipped in 1 day
  • AI-assisted motion design
  • Built as a single-page React app, with every page, interaction, and animation directed by Serena and implemented with Claude
  • Fully responsive, with dedicated mobile patterns: a bottom section nav with scroll progress, floating back buttons, and swipe gestures in galleries and book flippers
  • Dark mode that follows the visitor's device setting, with a manual toggle and a radial transition that expands from the toggle
  • Interactive details including a hover-to-reveal project preview panel, an animated journey timeline, a flippable book viewer, a fun-fact bubble, and a full-screen gallery slideshow
  • Performance work for slow connections: compressed images and videos, lazy loading, and fade-in on load
  • Made readable by AI tools and search engines: plain-text versions of every page, structured profile data, a sitemap, and an llms.txt summary
  • Old portfolio URLs automatically redirect to their new pages, with a custom 404 page listing current links
  • Deployed with GitHub Pages on a custom domain, with Google Analytics and Search Console set up

My journey

Childhood — Raised by a problem solver

My dad taught me to work smarter, not harder, and to design for real people in real contexts. When we kept losing our green garden tools in the grass, his fix was simple: tie on bright ribbons. I've been chasing better solutions ever since.

High school — Discovering design

I got into design in high school through a graphic design class. My teacher helped me get Adobe Creative Cloud over the summer, and I spent it working through every YouTube tutorial I could find.

High School / College — Exploring UX design

I picked up Adobe XD next, just to try it. Watching my static designs become interactive sparked my interest, and I fell in love with the whole UX process, from research to iteration.

Ronik Design — Working at an agency

My time at Ronik Design taught me to wear a lot of hats: graphic design, UX/UI, animation, often across several client projects at once. Constant feedback, revisions, and pitches made the work exciting and incredibly rewarding.

Now — Always learning and growing

Design is moving faster than ever. New tools are putting more power directly into designers' hands, and AI is already reshaping what UX even looks like.

I built this portfolio with Claude in a fraction of the usual time, so I've felt that shift first-hand. I'm excited for what's next and will keep exploring.
